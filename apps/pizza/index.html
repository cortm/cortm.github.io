At least 48 hrs ahead of eating…
  • 1
    Add 810.0 g water to a bowl and stir in 1.2 g yeast.
  • 2
    Add 1,200.0 g flour and 36.0 g salt.
  • 3
    Mix on low until no dry flour remains, about 2–3 minutes.
  • 4
    Cover in bowl (with baking wrap and towel) for 20 minutes.
  • 5
    Mix again on low to medium-low for 5–7 minutes, until smoother and elastic.
  • 6
    Cover in bowl for 20 minutes.
  • 7
    Do one stretch-and-fold of each corner.
  • 8
    Place in a lightly oiled covered container and refrigerate.

  • 2

  • 3

  • 4
    Cover with baking wrap and towel.
  • 5
    Stretch – when soft, increased in size, and room temperature.
